# MCT reading material

Downloaded public research sources and model descriptions. Original source links work on mobile. HTML files are page snapshots; extracted text is also saved alongside them. Page images and interactive charts may require an internet connection.

Suggested reading order: overview/FAQ, 2022 introduction, 2023 explanation, NY Fed 2024 paper, then Stock–Watson and its appendix.

## Measurement and Theory of Core Inflation — Almuzara and Sbordone (2024)

[Original source](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1115.pdf) | [Saved copy](nyfed_measurement_and_theory_core_inflation_2024.pdf)

NY Fed research paper; broader framework and discussion of MCT.

## Core Inflation and Trend Inflation — Stock and Watson (2016)

[Original source](https://www.princeton.edu/~mwatson/papers/core_and_trend_inflation_restat_2016.pdf) | [Saved copy](stock_watson_core_and_trend_inflation_2016.pdf)

Foundational published paper cited by the NY Fed; not a description of every modification in current MCT.

## Core Inflation and Trend Inflation — technical appendix

[Original source](https://www.princeton.edu/~mwatson/papers/core_and_trend_inflation_online_appendix_20151103.pdf) | [Saved copy](stock_watson_technical_appendix.pdf)

Technical background to Stock and Watson, including estimation details.

## NY Fed MCT overview and FAQ

[Original source](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/policy/mct) | [Saved copy](nyfed_mct_overview_faq.html)

Current official model description; web snapshot, not a publisher PDF.

## Inflation Persistence: How Much Is There and Where Is It Coming From?

[Original source](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2022/04/inflation-persistence-how-much-is-there-and-where-is-it-coming-from/) | [Saved copy](nyfed_inflation_persistence_introduction_2022.html)

Original introductory MCT article, April 2022.

## Where Is Inflation Persistence Coming From?

[Original source](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2023/07/where-is-inflation-persistence-coming-from/) | [Saved copy](nyfed_where_persistence_comes_from_2023.html)

Accessible explanation accompanying the July 2023 launch.
