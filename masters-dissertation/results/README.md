# Model results

One saved KOSPI 200 calibration CSV per model: 19 files in total.

- Five Levy models: Meixner, CGMY, Variance Gamma (VG), Normal Inverse Gaussian (NIG), and Generalised Hyperbolic (GH).
- Two BNS models: Gamma-OU (`k200_bns_calibration.csv`) and IG-OU (`k200_bns_ig_calibration.csv`).
- Twelve stochastic time-change models: Meixner, CGMY, VG, and NIG, each with CIR, Gamma-OU, and IG-OU clocks.

These are copies of the saved per-model calibration outputs from the original research folders. They have not been recalculated or checked against every reported value in the final dissertation.

Black-Scholes is also covered in the dissertation, but a standalone saved calibration CSV was not found. See the dissertation PDF for its reported results.
